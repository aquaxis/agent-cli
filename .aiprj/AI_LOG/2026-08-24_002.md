## Summary

Implemented the ability for agent-cli to **create a detached agent-cli process
that does not depend on the parent**. A running (or one-shot) agent-cli now
launches a headless, self-registering agent-cli in its own session; the child
outlives the launcher's exit, `Ctrl+C`, and terminal hang-up, serves peer
prompts over IPC, and is stoppable without a TTY.

Baseline before work: commit `6042abd`, `agent-cli` 0.3.0, 37 Rust files,
7 backend kinds. Finding that shaped the design: peers are **already**
independent (no parent/child lifetime tie) and there was **no** in-app spawn
feature, so the work was (a) a headless `serve` mode independent of stdin,
(b) a detached spawner using `setsid`, and (c) an IPC `Shutdown` + `stop` path.

## Design decisions / notes

- Independence rests on three facts: `setsid` (new session → immune to the
  parent's terminal SIGINT/SIGHUP), detached stdio, and the parent never holding
  the child with `kill_on_drop` nor `wait()`ing it.
- A headless agent cannot answer a y/N approval prompt, so `serve` forces
  auto-approve and never requests approval.
- The prologue was inlined into `run_headless` rather than factored out, to keep
  the delicate interactive `run` unchanged (design §4 / risk R-5).
