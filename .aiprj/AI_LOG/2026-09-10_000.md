## 3. Decisions worth recording

- **A role may have an empty palette entry.** "Default foreground" is not a
  colour to write — it is what is already on the stream — so `paint` returns the
  text untouched. This keeps P-3 (self-closing) trivially true for such a role:
  there is nothing to close.
- **The instruction's table is a reference, not a contract.** It asks for
  `bright black` + `dim` on exactly the rows changed in 2.3. Legibility on the
  user's actual terminal is what the table exists to serve, so the user's
  correction wins and the deviation is recorded as AS-11 rather than hidden.
- **Yellow 33, not 93, and no `dim`.** 33 is the darker of the two yellows, so
  the rows stay secondary without disappearing; `dim` was the source of the
  problem and is not carried over.
- **`Progress` and `Thinking` have genuinely diverged.** They shared their SGR
  parameters until now, which made design deviation D-4 (telling them apart by
  the reasoning rows' two-column indent) nominal. It is now load-bearing, and
  the indicator's block shows a dark-yellow status row above grey reasoning
  rows.
