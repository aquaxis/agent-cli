## 2. What was built

```text
> why is the sky blue?
[tool-call] bash {"command":"sleep 12"}
⠏ 5.8s
… +20 more (click to expand)
  reasoning line 20
  reasoning line 21
  …
  reasoning line 29
```

- `AgentEvent::Thinking` is no longer printed inline while the indicator is on:
  the display task feeds it to `StatusIndicator`, which buffers the turn's
  reasoning and renders its tail under the spinner.
- `editor::wrap_display` folds the text to the terminal width — wrapping, never
  cutting, so nothing is lost — and the last 10 rows are shown, indented, under
  a `… +N more (click to expand)` marker.
- A left click on the block toggles to as much of the reasoning as the screen
  can hold (`… +N more (click to collapse)`) and back. The choice persists
  across turns.
- Mouse reporting is enabled in `start()` and released in `finish()` /
  `abandon_run()`, with `DisableMouseCapture` in `RawModeGuard::drop` as a
  safety net — so it is on only while a turn runs, and only when reasoning is
  actually shown.
- The click is hit-tested by the pure `click_hits_indicator`, with the block
  located by one `cursor::position()` query (the display task is the last writer
  during a turn, so the cursor stands on the block's last row).

## 3. Decisions worth recording

| # | Decision | Reason |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | The expanded view is capped at `terminal_height - 5` rows, not literally everything. | An unbounded block scrolls the whole conversation off the screen; the cap keeps the activity line, spinner, marker and two rows of context. Recorded as AS-09. |
| 2 | Mouse reporting is gated on `[ui] show_thinking != "hidden"`. | With no reasoning there is nothing to click, and taking the terminal's drag-to-select away would be pure cost. FR-20 / AS-10. |
| 3 | A terminal that never answers `ESC[6n` falls back to treating any click during a turn as the toggle. | crossterm bounds the query at 2 s; degrading to a usable toggle beats doing nothing. R-13. |
| 4 | Reasoning is no longer kept in the scrollback while the indicator is on. | It is a live view of the running turn. The full text still goes to the conversation log, and `show_progress = false` restores the old inline output. AS-09. |
