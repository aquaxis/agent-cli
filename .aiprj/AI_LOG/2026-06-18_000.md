## Summary

Added `"opencode-go"` as a convenience provider kind that auto-configures OpenCode Go
cloud endpoint defaults. This allows users with an OpenCode Go subscription to
configure agent-cli with minimal TOML:

```toml
[provider]
kind = "opencode-go"

[provider.opencode]
api_key_env = "OPENCODE_API_KEY"
```

Instead of manually specifying the Go-specific `base_url`, `api`, and `api_key_env`.
