## 3. Findings (summary — full detail in the documents)

- **Cause**: a truncated provider stream body — the shared backend/relay
  resets or cuts one of the in-flight streaming responses under multi-agent
  concurrency (children inherit the parent's config chain and endpoint,
  BL-S9). The bare string is the *only* failure text the stream error
  branches print, so the observation pins the defect to that layer; it is not
  produced anywhere in the IPC/`send_to` plane, and a stopped child prints
  nothing (`Stdio::null`, BL-S9).
- **Correlation**: stop churn (the 500 ms abort drops an open body without a
  clean close, `app.rs:872-877`) plus the genuinely lossy stop path
  (queued prompts destroyed, `(no text response)` replies, 120 s ask
  timeouts — two such timeouts occurred live during this very session) make
  "the child stopped" and "the error appeared" land together, though the
  child is not the printer.
- **Solutions proposed (ranked, not implemented)**: (1) prefix the stream
  error with provider/model context and log error events into the
  conversation log; (2) graceful drain on stop — bounded grace, truthful
  replies to queued askers, then abort; (3) stop honesty — reject-during-stop
  reply and truthful `stopped` results; (4) `send_to` disambiguation of
  `peer stopped before replying`.
